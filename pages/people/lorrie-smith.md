bio: 
    Lorrie is a team member of the ISU Digital Repository, Iowa State’s institutional repository that provides free public access to research and scholarship by ISU faculty, students, and staff.


    Since 2014, Lorrie has worked with faculty in the areas of Life Sciences, Business, Design, Health and Human Sciences, and Liberal Arts and Sciences to deposit their publications in the Repository. She also helps to build profiles for new faculty in the Repository.


    In Lorrie’s off time, she enjoys spending time outside, riding her bicycle and camping.
